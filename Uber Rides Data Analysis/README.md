# Uber-Rides-Data-Analysis
Uber Rides Data Analysis Project

## INTRODUCTION

Uber Technologies, Inc., commonly known as Uber, is American technology company. Its services include ride-hailing, food delivery, package delivery, couriers, freight transportation, and through a partnership with Lime, electric bicycle and motorized scooter rental. 
This project focuses on analysing Uber ride data using Python and Jupyter Notebook to uncover ride patterns, customer behaviour, travel trends, and operational insights. The analysis includes data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization techniques to better understand ride activities.
The project demonstrates practical data analytics skills including:
•	Data Cleaning
•	Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
•	Data Visualization
•	Business Insight Generation

## OBJECTIVES

The main objectives of this project are:
•	Check how long do people travel with Uber?
•	What Hour Do Most People take Uber To Their Destination?
•	Check The purpose of trips
•	Which Day has the highest number of trips
•	What are the number of trips per each day?
•	What are the trips in the month?
•	The starting points of trips. Where do people start boarding their trip from Most?

## DATASET INFORMATION
The dataset contains Uber ride details such as:

`START_DATE`  Date and time when the trip started
`END_DATE`	  Date and time when the trip ended		
`CATEGORY`	  Type of ride (Business or Personal)
`START`	      Starting location of the ride
`STOP`		    Destination location
`MILES`  	    Distance travelled during the trip
`PURPOSE`	    Purpose of the ride (Meeting, Customer Visit, etc.)

### Key Insights
* Business rides make up the majority of Uber trips. 
* Most rides are short-distance trips. 
* Peak ride activity occurs during working hours and weekdays. 
* Certain months show higher ride frequency than others. 
* Meetings and customer visits are among the most common trip purposes. 
* Ride demand varies significantly by time and day.
  
### Business Insights
* Uber can optimize driver availability during peak business hours. 
* High business usage indicates strong corporate transportation demand. 
* Short-distance ride dominance suggests opportunities for urban mobility optimization. 
* Promotional campaigns can target low-demand periods to increase ride frequency. 
* Understanding ride purposes helps improve customer segmentation and service personalization. 
* Time-based demand analysis can support better pricing and operational strategies.
